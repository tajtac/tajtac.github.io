Two more of our papers on generative AI are now up on arXiv. The <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03409">first paper</a> gives a mechanics perspective on generative AI for material design using burgers as an example material. The <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08365">second one</a> investigates the role of texture on overall liking in AI-generated burgers.
